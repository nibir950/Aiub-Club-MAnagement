About Our Club

AIUB Computer Club is a student organization that focuses on programming, technology, innovation and skill development. Our club provides opportunities for students to learn new technologies, participate in competitions and work together on projects.

Mission of Computer Club

To develop students' technical skills, creativity and problem-solving abilities through different activities and workshops.

Our Vision

To build a community of skilled, innovative and technology-oriented students.

Our Objectives

  • Improve programming skills.
  • Organize technical workshops.
  • Encourage teamwork and collaboration.
  • Participate in programming competitions.
  • Develop real-world projects.
